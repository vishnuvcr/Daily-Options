# Phase 58 Literature Review

## Option order imbalance and subsequent index returns

Sensoy and Omole (2022) find significant relationships between index-option order imbalance and spot-index returns, including evidence of contemporaneous and predictive effects.

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1059056021002367

## Immediately-after-open option flow

Kang (2022) studies option order imbalance immediately after the market open and reports predictive content for stock-index and index-futures returns during the remainder of the day, with the effect driven mainly by institutional traders.

Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/fut.22301

## Put/call trading activity

Chordia et al. (2020/2021) show that index put order flow contains information about subsequent S&P 500 returns and that effects are stronger in higher-information environments.

Source: https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2019.3529

## Volume imbalance as a predictor

Michael, Cucuringu and Howison study normalized option-volume imbalance and find predictive information in aggregated option volumes, with stronger signals associated with market-maker volumes and some asymmetry between put and call information.

Source:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.09319

## Inventory versus information

Muravyev's research emphasizes that option order imbalance can be strongly affected by market-maker inventory risk as well as information. This is why Phase 58 treats any volume imbalance as a hypothesis variable and requires a strict after-cost gate.

Source:
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jofi.12380

## NIFTY-specific contextual evidence

A 2026 NIFTY high-frequency study examines put-call ratio, open-interest dynamics and intraday price discovery. It is used only as Indian-market context; the present phase uses volume rather than OI.

Source:
https://ijpub.org/ijvra/viewpaperforall.php?paper=IJVRA2607238

## Research implication

Phase 58 is a finite, reproducible public-data proxy test. It does not claim that call-vs-put volume is signed order flow.
