# Phase 52 Literature Review

## Implied volatility as a state variable

Carr and Wu derive option-implied, expected and realized volatility measures and report predictive information from volatility risk premia for subsequent stock returns.

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X16000052

Bollerslev, Gibson and Zhou estimate a time-varying volatility risk premium from option-implied and realized volatility and find temporal dependence plus predictive information for future stock returns.

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304407610000758

Bernales, Chen and Valenzuela discuss why volatility-risk-premium information can predict option returns and show the relationship in S&P 500 option data.

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165188917301434

Mixon studies index-option implied-volatility term structure and finds predictive information for future short-dated implied volatility.

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0927539806000715

## India/NIFTY context

Public Indian option analytics routinely report ATM IV as a core NIFTY volatility state variable alongside India VIX and realized volatility. This is practitioner context only and is not treated as profitability evidence.

Reference: https://www.oidata.in/

## Research implication

Phase 52 isolates the 09:30 ATM-IV level as a state variable observable immediately before the 09:31 trade. It therefore avoids the prior-session ATM quote-completeness problem that made Phase 51 data-limited while preserving strict timing and the same economic promotion gate.
