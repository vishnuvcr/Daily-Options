# Phase 55 Literature Review

Mixon (2007), The implied volatility term structure of stock index options, finds that ATM IV slope across maturities has some predictive ability for future short-dated implied volatility and emphasizes the importance of volatility risk premia.

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0927539806000715

Wang & Yen (2019), The information content of the implied volatility term structure on future returns, find term-structure information significant and complementary to IV level for future S&P 500 excess returns.

Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/eufm.12166

Shaikh & Padhi (2014) document Nifty implied-volatility smile/skew and term-structure patterns across moneyness and time-to-expiration.

Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/265969341_Stylized_patterns_of_implied_volatility_in_India_A_case_study_of_NSE_Nifty_options

Dixit, Yadav & Jain study the expectations hypothesis using NIFTY index-option data and report mean reversion plus departures from the simple rational-expectations pattern in longer-dated option IV.

Source: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1103428

The literature supports a bounded test of current-session IV term structure but does not establish an after-cost intraday NIFTY options edge. The frozen dual-friction gate therefore remains the decisive criterion.
