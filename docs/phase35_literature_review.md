# Phase 35 — Literature Review

## Cross-market transmission
Phase 31.8 established that a simple prior-session global-return directional signal did not survive the project's weekly economic gate. Phase 35 therefore tests whether global shocks interact with a local volatility state rather than assuming a constant directional effect.

## Implied versus realized volatility
The variance-risk-premium literature documents systematic differences between option-implied and subsequently realized variance. The IV-RV gap is therefore a documented volatility-state variable, but its use here is an empirical hypothesis rather than an assertion of profitability.

## Regime dependence
Volatility-risk compensation and option-market behavior vary across market regimes. Conditioning a cross-market shock on the local IV-RV state is intended to test this interaction directly while keeping the signal family small enough to avoid post-result data mining.

## Methodological implication
The Phase 35 grid is frozen before numerical results. It cannot be expanded to alternative IV lookbacks, thresholds, option structures or additional global markets in response to observed outcomes.
