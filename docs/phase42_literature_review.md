# Phase 42 literature review

## Primary evidence
- Bhat, A., Pandey, P., & Nageswara Rao, S. V. D. (2024), *The asymmetry in day and night option returns: Evidence from an emerging market*, Journal of Futures Markets, 44(8), 1320–1337. DOI 10.1002/fut.22512.
  - Reports positive and significant overnight returns accompanied by negative intraday returns in short NIFTY option strategies.
  - Reports that the asymmetry weakens on large-jump days.
  - Public data availability is stated through Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10899828.
- Research dataset: Bhat, *Nifty spot, futures and options one-minute data from 2017 to 2020*, Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10899828.
- Broad literature: *Why do option returns change sign from day to night?* documents day/night asymmetry in index and equity options and notes that transaction costs matter for implementation.

## Relevance and limitation
The literature motivates testing the state variable but does not test the preregistered Phase 42 rule, its opening-gap conditioning, historical NIFTY lot sizes, current cost model, or the ₹5,000/week promotion criterion. Therefore the literature is hypothesis-generating rather than performance evidence.

## Research gap
The Phase 42 experiment asks whether a prior-session option-return asymmetry state adds conditional directional information to the next session's opening-gap response, using a fixed one-lot debit-spread implementation and explicit friction stress.
