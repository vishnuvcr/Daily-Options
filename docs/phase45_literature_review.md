# Phase 45 Literature Review

Recent NIFTY gap research reports that gap behaviour differs between continuation and reversal contexts and that the first minutes after the open contain information about whether an opening move persists. A 2026 NIFTY first-15-minute study reports different continuation/reversal frequencies by the direction of the first candle, while 2026 gap studies emphasize that the same-day behaviour of a gap depends on context and magnitude. These are hypothesis-generating observations; Phase 45 tests a fixed event-confirmation state with full option friction rather than assuming a profitable direction.

Sources: Intraday Lab, “What Happens After Nifty's First 15-Minute Candle?” (2026); Intraday Lab, “Nifty Gap Down History: What Happens After a Panic Open?” (2026).
