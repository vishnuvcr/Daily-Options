# Phase 50 Literature Review

## Full versus partial opening gaps

The open-gap literature distinguishes partial gaps (opening inside the prior day's range) from full gaps (opening beyond the prior high/low). The 2023 open-access study on S&P 500, Nasdaq100 and Russell 2000 stocks explicitly studies these two gap types and reports different subsequent price-adjustment behavior.

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10017064/

## Acceptance/rejection of the prior range

A current 2026 Indian-market research article proposes classifying gaps according to whether the opening is inside the prior range or outside it and then observing acceptance/rejection. This is hypothesis-generation context only, not NIFTY options profitability evidence.

Source: https://www.tradefirm.in/blogs/gap-up-gap-down-index-plan

## Gap continuation/reversal

Brooks's treatment of gap openings frames large gap openings as breakout-like events but cautions that early behavior can alternate between continuation and range response.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119202622.ch20

## Research implication

A prior-range-location state is economically different from raw gap magnitude and from the prior-session directional-return state tested in Phase 48. Phase 50 therefore provides a distinct, finite test of whether full versus partial gap structure affects after-cost NIFTY option outcomes.
