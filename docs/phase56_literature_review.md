# Phase 56 Literature Review

## Opening gaps and context

Sanchez et al.'s opening-gap study separates full and partial gaps and examines subsequent price adjustment, supporting the broader hypothesis that opening-gap context can matter for the post-open path.

Source:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10017064/

Brooks' gap-opening chapter discusses continuation and reversal as competing outcomes and emphasizes that gap behavior depends on surrounding price-action context.

Source:
https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119202622.ch20

## Candle/body information

OHLC-based research evaluates whether simple price-structure features can carry intraday directional content and also demonstrates a recurring economic limitation: small directional effects can be overwhelmed by friction. This is directly relevant to the project because every Phase 56 result is evaluated after brokerage, statutory charges and conservative slippage.

Source:
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404476167_Structural_Limits_of_OHLCV-Based_Intraday_Signals_in_MNQ_Futures_A_Systematic_Falsification_Study

## Indian/NIFTY context

Current Indian gap research treats the relationship between the prior range, opening location and continuation/reversal as context rather than a deterministic signal. These practitioner sources are used only as hypothesis-generation material.

Sources:
