# Phase 43 Literature Review

Recent NIFTY research reports that day-of-week effects can vary across market regimes rather than remaining stable. Dhankhar et al. (2025) examine Nifty 50 weekday effects with rolling windows and GARCH/EGARCH methods and report time-varying patterns. Sahoo (2021) reports different weekday effects before versus during the COVID-19 period. These findings motivate a preregistered weekday interaction test without selecting a weekday after observing returns. The present phase is an options-execution test of a calendar state, not a claim that any particular weekday is profitable.

Sources: Dhankhar et al., Cogent Economics & Finance (2025), DOI 10.1080/23322039.2025.2571402; Sahoo, Journal of Public Affairs (2021), DOI 10.1002/pa.2621.
