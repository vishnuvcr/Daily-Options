# Phase 37 Literature Review — Global Shock × NIFTY Opening Dislocation

### Opening gaps contain information distinct from close-to-close returns
Wang et al. (2009) decompose daily returns into overnight and daytime components and study their different statistical properties. The separation motivates treating the NIFTY 09:30 opening gap as a distinct information variable rather than treating it as ordinary close-to-close return. https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.79.056109

Hong & Wang's work on periodic market closures shows that open-to-open returns are more volatile than close-to-close returns and that trading activity/price formation changes around the open and close. This supports explicit timing around the opening boundary. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/0022-1082.00207

### Foreign overnight information and opening gaps
De Gooijer, Diks and Gatarek study forecasting of close-to-open returns in global indices from overnight foreign-market price patterns and explicitly organize the information set by geographic market clusters. This is closely related to the Phase 37 global-shock/opening-gap mechanism. https://ideas.repec.org/a/psc/journl/v4y2012i1p23-44.html

A recent 2026 Borsa Istanbul Review paper studies opening-gap forecasting for 24 global equity indices using time-respecting cross-index information. It finds that opening-gap predictability is selective and depends on market position in the global trading sequence. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214845026000918

### Overnight shocks and short-term reversals
A 2026 Journal of Behavioral and Experimental Finance article reports short-term return reversals after overnight jump returns in a large US equity sample. While its asset universe differs from NIFTY options, it motivates testing whether a large overnight/global shock can be followed by a short-horizon reversal rather than automatic continuation. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214635026000821

### International spillovers
Diebold & Yilmaz document economically important international return and volatility spillovers; the Phase 37 design retains the strict non-overlapping global-to-NIFTY information barrier used in Phase 31.8. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1468-0297.2008.02208.x

## Research gap
Prior research studies opening gaps and global information flows separately or across broad asset universes. Phase 37 freezes a directly tradable NIFTY-options hypothesis: whether the *alignment or divergence* between a prior global shock and the NIFTY 09:30 gap changes the next short-horizon directional spread outcome after realistic costs.

## Methodological implication
The global block-permutation null destroys the date alignment between the six foreign-market returns and NIFTY outcomes while preserving cross-market dependence inside each permuted global vector. The local NIFTY opening-gap variable is held fixed in the null so that the test isolates incremental information in global timing.
