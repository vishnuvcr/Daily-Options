# Phase 56 Literature Review

## Equity-index volatility term structure

Mixon (2007) tests expectations-hypothesis implications for implied-volatility term structures of stock-index options and finds that ATM IV slope across maturities contains some predictive information for future short-dated implied volatility, while time-varying volatility risk premia affect the interpretation.

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0927539806000715

## Indian index-option term structure

Dixit, Yadav and Jain (2007) directly study S&P CNX NIFTY index-option implied-volatility term structures. They report mean reversion and deviations of long-dated implied volatility from what a simple rational-expectations relationship would imply.

Source: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1103428

## NIFTY volatility-surface context

Shaikh and Padhi (2014) study NIFTY option smile, skew and term structure, documenting systematic variation with time-to-expiration and moneyness.

Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/265969341_Stylized_patterns_of_implied_volatility_in_India_a_case_study_of_NSE_Nifty_options

Sehgal and Narayanamurthy (2008) also study NIFTY implied-volatility shape and its determinants.

Source: https://ejournal.usm.my/aamjaf/article/view/aamjaf_vol4-no1-2008_3

## Research implication

The literature supports current-session term structure as a distinct state variable. It does not establish a profitable directional NIFTY option strategy after Indian costs, so Phase 56 uses a finite tercile grid, fixed permutation nulls and the existing Base/Stress promotion gate.
