# Phase 54 Literature Review

The 2025 Journal of Financial Economics paper on option-implied information discusses the predictive content of implied-volatility spread and skew and emphasizes that financing/borrow frictions can explain part of the apparent signal.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2025.104153

Doran and Krieger study future asset returns linked to call-put volatility differences and the left side of the implied-volatility skew, providing direct motivation for isolating the ATM put-call spread.

Source: https://doi.org/10.2469/faj.v66.n1.9

Fu et al. document return predictability from option-implied volatility measures, including call-put spreads and skew, across multiple horizons.

Source: https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/id/eprint/80351/

These studies motivate the bounded Phase-54 hypothesis only. They do not establish after-cost profitability for intraday NIFTY options.
