# Phase 48 Literature Review

## Prior return and opening gap as separate short-horizon signals

A study of TOPIX futures and 10-year JGB futures explicitly compares predictive information from a prior one-day return measure and an opening-gap return measure, using cumulative-return and nonparametric tests. This is directly relevant to Phase 48 because the phase asks whether the interaction of prior-session direction and current opening-gap direction contains information not captured by the gap alone.

Source:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S104402830600007X

## Gap continuation and reversal

Plastun et al. (2020) analyze price gaps in the DJI, S&P 500 and NASDAQ and report abnormal post-gap movement with a temporary momentum component. The result motivates testing both FOLLOW and FADE rather than assuming every gap fills.

Source:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1062940820300747

A 2023 open-access study of S&P 500, Nasdaq100 and Russell 2000 stocks studies opening gaps as responses to overnight information and finds that subsequent adjustment differs by gap direction and event context.

Source:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10017064/

## Indian/NIFTY context

Current NIFTY gap research and trading material frequently combines gap direction with prior-session market structure or direction. These sources are useful hypothesis-generation context only; they are not treated as evidence of after-cost profitability for NIFTY options.

## Research implication

Phase 48 is a finite interaction test, not a model-selection exercise. The simple directional state is fixed before any P&L is observed, and all economic evaluation uses the repository's realistic Base/Stress friction model.
