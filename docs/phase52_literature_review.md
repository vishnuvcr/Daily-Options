# Phase 52 Literature Review

## Volatility level as a state variable

Volatility-risk-premium research documents time variation in implied volatility and predictive information in volatility-related state variables. Carr and Wu develop volatility-risk-premium measures from option-implied and realized/expected volatility; Bollerslev, Gibson and Zhou document time-varying volatility risk premia and predictive information for future returns.

## Why same-session 09:30 IV is a separate hypothesis

The earlier prior-session ATM-IV test was data-limited, not economically negative. Measuring IV at 09:30 tests whether the contemporaneous option-implied volatility state, after the opening gap has occurred but before the 09:31 trade, contains directional information that a prior-day state cannot capture.

Because 09:30 is before the registered 09:31 entry, the information barrier remains explicit.

## Gap context

Gap studies show that opening-gap continuation and reversal can differ by event context and gap magnitude. The phase keeps the gap direction mapping fixed and asks only whether the 09:30 IV state conditions its subsequent payoff.

## Research implication

Phase 52 is a bounded state test, not a parameter search. If coverage passes, the 12-cell result is judged only by the preregistered dual-friction weekly gate.
