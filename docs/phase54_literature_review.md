# Phase 54 Literature Review

Option-implied volatility spreads and skew can contain information about later returns, but multiple studies show their apparent economic value can be sensitive to construction and trading costs.

- Jha & Kalimipalli (2010) study conditional skewness in index options and explicitly discuss the weakening effect of trading costs.
  https://doi.org/10.1002/fut.20414
- Ulrich & Walther (2020) show that option-implied variance/skew measures are sensitive to volatility-surface construction, supporting a deliberately simple matched-strike CE/PE IV spread.
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11147-020-09166-0
- Yu, Huang & Zhou (2025) find predictive information in option-implied skewness for aggregate equity returns; the horizon and market are different from this intraday NIFTY test.
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0927539825000647
- Shaikh & Padhi (2014) document persistent Nifty implied-volatility smile/skew structure across moneyness and maturity.
  https://www.researchgate.net/publication/265969341_Stylized_patterns_of_implied_volatility_in_India_A_case_study_of_NSE_Nifty_options

These sources motivate the hypothesis only. Phase 54 uses the project's fixed after-cost promotion gate and does not import external performance estimates.
